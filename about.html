ABOUT THE SPACE

Talk clearly.
Stay close.

rolandvideochat is a simple place to catch up with a few people. Make a room, send the code to your friends, and start talking. Nobody needs to make an account.

It is meant for the everyday calls that do not need a complicated meeting setup.

Keep it small.

Each room holds up to four people. That is enough space for everyone to be part of the conversation, without a wall of tiny faces or a list of features to work through.

You stay in control.

A room is reached through its code and disappears when the last person leaves. You can also make it public when you want other people to find it.
